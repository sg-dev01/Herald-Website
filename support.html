Common questions

A walkout song won't play

Imported songs must be saved on your iPhone, not only in iCloud Drive. Open the file once in the Files app, then try again.

Apple Music songs need a connection and an active Apple Music subscription.

The announcer says a name wrong

In Edit Player, respell the name the way it sounds, then regenerate. Fixing an existing clip is free, up to 5 times per player.

Does Gameday work without signal?

Yes. Generated intros and announcements, imported songs, and downloaded Gameday Tracks all play offline. Generating new voice clips and playing Apple Music need a connection.

No sound through a Bluetooth speaker

Reconnect the speaker in iPhone Settings, under Bluetooth. Some speakers drop the first second of audio after sitting idle, so play a test before the first batter.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Gameday. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My subscription is missing on a new phone

Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with. In Gameday, open the subscription screen from Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my account?

In Gameday, open Settings and choose Delete My Account. This doesn't cancel your subscription, so cancel that separately.

Reporting a problem

Include these in your email so we can fix it faster:

  • What you tapped and what happened
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • Your Gameday version, from Settings, under About
